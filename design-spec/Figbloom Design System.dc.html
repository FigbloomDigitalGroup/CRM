03 · TYPE

Instrument Sans stays. The frozen sign-in pages use it, it’s compact enough for tables and has a confident bold for headings. IBM Plex Mono is added only for table headers, section labels and IDs (it echoes the monospace headers already in the app) so numbers and labels separate cleanly from content.

08 · MOTION AND INTERACTION
  • Enter 160–200 ms ease (menus fade-up 8px, drawers slide 28px). No motion on data changes beyond opacity.
  • Hover: tint one step (surface → surface-2). Pressed: primary-hover. Focus: 2px orange ring, 2px offset, always visible on keyboard.
  • Every destructive or stage-changing action gives an Undo toast for 5 seconds.
  • Hit targets: 40px desktop minimum, 44px on mobile; tab bar items 48px tall.
