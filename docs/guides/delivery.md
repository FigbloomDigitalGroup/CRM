# Delivery quick guide

## Your first 10 minutes

1. Sign in with your own email and password.
2. Read your Home. It shows what is waiting for you.
3. Open **Handoffs**. Open one that was passed to you and read the deal summary.
4. Open **Tasks** and **Calendar** to see your own work.
5. Open a company from **Companies** to see its services, contacts and history.

## Your daily routine

1. Check Home and Handoffs for new work.
2. Work through the onboarding tasks of each new handoff.
3. Add a note after every contact with the customer.
4. When the work is done, mark the handoff **Delivered**. The company then becomes an active customer.
5. Check tomorrow's tasks.

## The five things you can do

1. **Receive a handoff** and read what was sold: the service, the contacts, the notes.
2. **Mark a handoff Delivered** when the work is finished.
3. **Keep tasks and the calendar** for your own work.
4. **Read the customer's page** (Customer 360): services, contacts, history.
5. **Add notes and attachments**, and take part in conversations with the customer.

## What you cannot see, and why

| You do not see | Why |
|---|---|
| Price, value, margin or cost of a deal | Hidden by role. You see what was sold, not what it cost or earned. |
| Open deals and the pipeline | You see won deals only. |
| Leads | Leads belong to the sales team. |
| Payments | Only Finance and Management. |
| Settings, audit log, organisation reports | Management only. You have **My metrics**. |

If a page says "no access", read who has access and ask them.
