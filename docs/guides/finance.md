# Finance quick guide

## Your first 10 minutes

1. Sign in with your own email and password.
2. Read your Home. It shows what is overdue, what is awaiting payment and what still needs an invoice.
3. Open **Payments**. There is one row per won deal, with its contract, paid and balance.
4. Open a row to see its invoices.
5. Open a company's page and look at its payments.

## Your daily routine

1. Open Payments and the **Overdue** tab first.
2. Add a follow-up note on each overdue invoice, with a date.
3. Mark invoices as sent when you send them.
4. Record each payment as it arrives.
5. Check the Awaiting payment tab before you finish.

## The five things you can do

1. **Track the money of every won deal**: contract, invoiced, paid, balance.
2. **Mark an invoice sent** and **record a payment**. A blank amount means the full balance. Use "Mark paid in full" when it all arrived.
3. **Reverse a wrong payment.** A reason is required. The payment stays on the record, struck through.
4. **Void an invoice** with a reason, if no payment is recorded on it.
5. **Leave a follow-up note** on a payment or a customer for the person who has to chase it.

The CRM does not process money. A payment's method is a plain label. Never type account or M-Pesa details into it.

## What you cannot see, and why

| You do not see | Why |
|---|---|
| Leads and the open pipeline | You see won deals only. |
| Margins and costs | Hidden by role. |
| Tasks you can assign to others | Finance uses follow-up notes instead of tasks. |
| Settings and the audit log | Management only. Inside an invoice you are told the audit history is read by Management. |

If a page says "no access", it names who to ask.
