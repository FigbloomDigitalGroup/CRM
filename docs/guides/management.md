# Management quick guide

You run the organisation in the CRM. You see everything, so you also carry the duty to keep it accurate. The detail of running it is in the [Admin guide](admin.md).

## Your first 10 minutes

1. Sign in. Read your Home. It shows open tasks, what is overdue, new leads, stalled deals and how much the team uses the CRM.
2. If this is a new organisation, follow the [first-run checklist](first-run-checklist.md).
3. Open **Settings** and check Organization profile and Members.
4. Open **Reports**. Pick last month and read lead sources and win rate.
5. Open **Preferences** and choose your own look.

## Your daily routine

1. Read Home: stalled deals and overdue tasks first.
2. Check new leads are assigned.
3. Open any deal that needs a decision.
4. Look at Payments for overdue invoices.
5. Once a week, read Reports and the usage figures on Home.

## The five things you can do

1. **Run the team**: invite members, change roles, deactivate leavers.
2. **See every lead, deal, customer and payment**, including values and margins.
3. **Read organisation Reports** and export them.
4. **Set up the CRM** in Settings: lists, templates, automations, website form.
5. **Check what happened** in the audit log, and control security and privacy.

## What you cannot do

- You cannot act on your own membership (for example deactivate yourself).
- You cannot remove a person who still owns open records until you hand those over.
- Automations never move a deal stage, set a discount or approve anything for you. They assign, remind and draft only.
- There are five roles. Members who need help use Help, then Contact support.
