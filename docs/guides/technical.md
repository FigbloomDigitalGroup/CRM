# Restricted Technical quick guide

This role has a small, focused view on purpose. It shows the work given to you and nothing about money.

## Your first 10 minutes

1. Sign in with your own email and password.
2. Open **My work**. It lists the tasks assigned to you and the conversations you are on.
3. Open a task and read the customer requirement that comes with it.
4. Mark a task done when it is finished.
5. Open **Preferences** to choose your look and notices.

## Your daily routine

1. Open My work.
2. Do the oldest task first.
3. Add a note when something changes.
4. Check your notifications for anything new that mentions you.

## The five things you can do

1. **See your assigned work** and the requirements for it.
2. **Complete and create tasks** for your own work.
3. **Add notes** to the work you are on.
4. **Read conversations** you are part of.
5. **Get notifications** on your computer and phone.

## What you cannot see, and why

| You do not see | Why |
|---|---|
| Deals, values, margins | Hidden by role. |
| Companies and customer lists | Not part of this role. You see only the work given to you. |
| Payments | Only Finance and Management. |
| Reports, settings, audit log | Not part of this role. |

If a page says "no access", it names who to ask.
