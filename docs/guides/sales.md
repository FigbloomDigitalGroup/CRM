# Sales quick guide

## Your first 10 minutes

1. Sign in with your own email and password. Open the CRM on your phone too and add it to the home screen (More, then Install app).
2. Read **Today**. It lists what to do first: overdue tasks, tasks due today and new leads.
3. Open **Leads**. Open one and read its history.
4. Press `N` to add a new lead. Only the name is required. You can fill in the rest later.
5. Open **Preferences** to choose your look and which notices you get.

## Your daily routine

1. Open Today. Work down the list from the top.
2. Reply to new leads first.
3. Log every call or message. After a call from a contact, the CRM asks "How did it go?". Answer it. That is your note.
4. Set a next step on every lead and deal before you leave it.
5. Before you finish, check Tasks for tomorrow.

## The five things you can do

1. **Capture a lead.** From your computer or the phone Capture button. On the phone it works without signal and sends when you are back online. A likely duplicate warns you. It never stops you saving.
2. **Qualify a lead and turn it into a deal.** Fill the qualification questions, then convert.
3. **Work your deals.** Drag a deal between stages on a computer. Add notes, attachments and a next step.
4. **Hand over a won deal.** Start the handoff so Delivery knows what to deliver.
5. **Keep tasks.** Every task links to a lead, deal, company or contact. Use the calendar for the week.

You can also search (Ctrl/Cmd K), add contacts and companies, and keep renewal dates on a customer.

## What you cannot see, and why

| You do not see | Why |
|---|---|
| Other people's leads and deals | You see your own, plus a shared pool of unassigned leads you can claim (take ownership). You cannot edit someone else's record. |
| A deal value on a deal you do not own, and margins and costs | They are hidden by role. A hidden value shows as "Hidden", not a blank. |
| Payments | Only Finance and Management see payments. |
| Settings, the audit log, imports and bulk exports | Management only. |
| Organisation reports | You get **My metrics**: your own numbers. |

If a page says "no access", it tells you who to ask. Moving a deal to another stage is not possible from the phone, so nothing is overwritten while you are offline. Use a computer for that.
