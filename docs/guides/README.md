Customer Support is a proposed role, switched off by default. It has no guide until it is switched on for an organisation.

## Rules every guide follows

- A guide only describes what that role can see. If a role cannot see a number, the guide does not show one.
- Deal values, margins and costs are hidden by role. Payments are visible only to Finance and Management. Where you see "Hidden" in the CRM, that is on purpose.
- When you open a page you may not use, the CRM tells you who has access and who to ask. It is not a fault.

## Help inside the CRM

- Press `?` anywhere, or open Help, for tips on the page you are on and the keyboard shortcuts.
- "What's new" lists recent changes.
- Press Ctrl/Cmd K to search for anything you are allowed to open.

## What these guides do not include

Walk-through videos and the attendance record need people. See the end of the [training plan](training-plan.md).
