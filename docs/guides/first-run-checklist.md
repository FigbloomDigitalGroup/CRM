# First-run checklist (setting up a new organisation)

For Management. The CRM also shows a short four-step version on the Management home. This is the longer one. Tick each box as you go.

## Before you start
- [ ] You can sign in as Management with your own email.
- [ ] You know the people who will use it and which role each should have (see the [Admin guide](admin.md)).
- [ ] You have your existing customer list in a spreadsheet (`.csv` or `.xlsx`, up to 20 MB), if you want to import it.

## Set up (about 30 minutes)
- [ ] **Organization profile**: name, phone, address, time zone, working days and hours, logo.
- [ ] **Reference data**: check lead sources, services and lost reasons match how you work.
- [ ] **Invite your team**: Settings, Members. Name, email, role. Ask each person to accept the email.
- [ ] **Import existing data**: Settings, Data migration. Try a small sample first and read the dry run.
- [ ] **Connect the website form**: Settings, Website integration. Copy the key when shown (it appears once) and give it to whoever manages the website. Check the state changes to Connected after a test lead.
- [ ] **Inbound email** (if used): create the address and test it.
- [ ] **Automations**: read the list and switch on the reminders you want.
- [ ] **Security**: check the session length. Read the role table.

## For each new user (first sign-in)
- [ ] They accepted the invite and signed in.
- [ ] They opened Preferences and chose their look and notifications.
- [ ] They installed the CRM on their phone (More, then Install app).
- [ ] They did the first-10-minutes list in their role guide.
- [ ] They added or opened one real record.

## Check it works
- [ ] A test lead from the website appears in Leads and is assigned.
- [ ] Sales can open a lead and cannot open Payments or Settings, and the message says who to ask.
- [ ] Delivery sees a handoff without any price.
- [ ] Finance can open Payments.
- [ ] You can see the changes in the audit log.

## Done when
Everyone who should use the CRM has signed in once, your data is in, and the website form is Connected.

## About the in-app checklist
The four-step checklist on the Management home ticks a step when you open it, and remembers that on this computer only. It does not check that the step was really finished. Use this page to confirm.
